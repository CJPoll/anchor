## Deviations from the QA plan, and why

- **Operators rows 1 and 2.** The plan says row 1's `delete_statement` gives
  "none for `y = g(x)` (y used later)" and row 2's `drop_effect_call` gives "one
  edit removing the `Logger.info(y)` line". Both are pinned at two levels: the
  operator makes the edit (`y = g(x)`; the Logger line, whole), and the
  generator refuses it. Row 2's mutant would leave `y` unused, a compiler
  warning, so requirement 2 (every mutant compiles under
  `--warnings-as-errors`) refuses it too; the test pins the reason
  `leaves variable \`y\` bound at line 5 unused`.
- **Row 8 (`with u <- fetch(id)`).** The operator returns the edit with the
  skip reason `the pattern has nothing to relax`; the generator lists it in
  `skipped`, never as a mutant.
